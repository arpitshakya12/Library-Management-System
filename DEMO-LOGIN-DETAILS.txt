DEMO DATA BEHAVIOUR
- Sample books and transactions are inserted automatically on first visit.
- The 10 sample students are added if missing, including when upgrading from an older demo ZIP; existing/custom student records are preserved.
- Existing non-empty LocalStorage records are preserved.
- Admin can edit/delete sample books and students like normal records.
- Changes remain in the same browser's LocalStorage after refresh.
- To restore the original sample data for a fresh demonstration, clear site data for this website in the browser and reload.

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end academic demo. Authentication and passwords are stored in browser LocalStorage and are not suitable for real-world production use.
